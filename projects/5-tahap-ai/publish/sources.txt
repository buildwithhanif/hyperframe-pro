5-tahap-kreator-ai: provenance of everything on screen and on the soundtrack

Script: the five stage lines are the requester's own words, verbatim on screen.
Added copy (hook, stage call-outs, labels, CTA wording) written for this build from the requester's brief:
"belajar bikin system konten pake 1-on-1 bareng @hanifproduktif / cek link di bio / limited 3 participants/week".

Voice-over: synthesized locally. Piper voice id_ID-news_tts-medium (sherpa-onnx release "tts-models",
github.com/k2-fsa/sherpa-onnx), pitch/formant lowered with pyworld. Not a real person's voice.
Intelligibility checked line by line with Whisper small (sherpa-onnx release "asr-models").

Visuals: all drawn in code (kreator.mjs, build.mjs). No screenshots, no generated images, no footage.
The "posts" are abstract drawn cards and do not imitate any real app.

Fonts (SIL Open Font License, via npm @fontsource 5.3.0), licences in assets/fonts/OFL-*.txt:
  Bricolage Grotesque 800 (also as outlines in assets/glyphs.json), Plus Jakarta Sans 600/800, Caveat 700.
GSAP 3.14.2 (npm gsap): assets/vendor/gsap.min.js, vendored because the render browser has no CDN access.

Music and SFX: synthesized from scratch by audio.py (numpy). No samples.
